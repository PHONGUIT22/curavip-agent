## 🎬 Minute-by-Minute Script

### Act 1: The Hook & The "Cultural Blindness" Problem (00:00 - 00:35)
- **Visual**: Screen opens on CuraVIP's Dark Luxury Console (Obsidian black and shimmering Champagne Gold). The agent console is ready.
- **Narrator**:
  > *"Every day, multi-million dollar deals are made or broken over dinners and gifts. Yet today, when dealmakers ask foundation AI models how to impress a high-profile partner, AI fails catastrophically with 'cultural blindness'.*
  > *Ask ChatGPT or Claude ungrounded, and it suggests a cliché: a generic Montblanc pen, a fruit basket, or a Napa Cabernet—even if your partner is a teetotaler or an avant-garde architecture connoisseur.*

### Act 2: The "Grounding Toggle" — Side-by-Side Live Proof (00:35 - 01:25)
- **Visual**: Narrator clicks the **"Side-by-Side"** button on the Top Navigation Bar, or toggles the **"Qloo Grounding"** switch from ON to OFF and back to ON. The split comparison modal appears.
- **Narrator**:
  > *"To show you why Qloo is indispensable, look at this Grounding Toggle.*
  > *On the left is the **Generic LLM baseline**: for Marcus Vance, a fund manager who loves Christopher Nolan and Brutalism, the generic model recommends a generic movie box set and a mainstream steakhouse.*

### Act 3: Autonomous Personas & FCPA Compliance Guardrails (01:25 - 02:10)
- **Visual**: Switch principal in the Left Rail to **Tariq Al-Mansoor** (Founder from Riyadh). Click **"Synthesize Dossier"**.
- **Narrator**:
  > *"Now let's examine sovereign dealmaking with Tariq Al-Mansoor. Tariq is an independent horology collector who adheres to a strict zero-alcohol and halal protocol.*

### Act 4: Model Context Protocol (MCP) & Multimodal Delivery (02:10 - 02:40)
